**Extended Data Fig. f | WhatsHap haplotag assignment yield across sequencing depth.**
The percentage of processed alignments assigned to haplotype 1 or haplotype 2 is
shown for HG002 across three sequencing platforms, two mappers and 10×, 30× and 50×
depth. Each point is one deterministic workflow output; lines connect adjacent
measured depths and do not represent fitted trends. Minimap2 is shown by solid lines
and filled circles, and winnowmap by dashed lines and open circles. Taggable rate is
defined as 100 × (H1 + H2) / alignments processed. Values are alignment-based, not
unique-read counts or truth-validated haplotype assignments. H1/H2 ratios ranged from
1.0033 to 1.0287 across the 18 workflows and are provided in Source Data.
